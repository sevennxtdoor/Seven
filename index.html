Features

Glance up. It's all there.

The pill shows one live thing at a time. Hover it and it opens into pages you swipe through with two fingers on your touchpad.

Now playing

Cover, title and a live equalizer from Spotify, YouTube in your browser, or any app that uses the Windows media controls.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that follow the song line by line, right in the pill, so you can sing along without opening anything.

Timer & stopwatch

A countdown that stays visible while you work, and a gentle alarm when it's done.

Notifications

Messages slide into the pill as small banners, and the recent ones wait for you on their own page.

Volume & brightness

Sliders for both in one place, plus a clean indicator in the pill whenever the volume changes.

Weather & calendar

Today's temperature, how it feels outside and the day's high and low, next to a small calendar.

To-do & water

A short to-do list, and a water tracker that counts your glasses toward a daily goal.

Shelf

Drop files on the pill to keep them close, then drag them back out into any app when you need them.

Polite by design

Knows when to step aside.

Seven is built to be there when you look for it and gone when you don't.

A face when it's quiet

With nothing going on, the pill shows two small eyes that blink now and then, and fall asleep when you step away.

Out of the way in fullscreen

Watching a video or playing a game? The pill shrinks to a thin colored line. Behind a maximized window it hides completely.

Peek any time

Hold your cursor at the top edge of the screen and it comes back, even when it's hidden.

Smooth on high refresh rates

Spring animations drawn by the Windows compositor, so it moves fluidly on 120 and 144 Hz screens too.

Privacy & safety

Your desktop stays yours.

No account, no tracking, no server of its own. Here's exactly what Seven does on your PC.

  • No admin rights. It installs into your own user folder and uninstalls cleanly from Settings → Apps.
  • Your data stays local. To-dos, water count and shelf items live in one file, %AppData%\Seven\data.json. Shelf keeps links to your files, never copies.
  • It never reads your keyboard. Volume and brightness come straight from the Windows audio and display settings, not from your keys.
  • Notifications only with your permission. Windows asks you first. They're shown in the pill and never sent anywhere.
  • Starting with Windows is your choice. It's a checkbox in the installer you can untick, and the uninstaller removes it.

FAQ

Questions

Is Seven free?

Yes, completely free to use at home or at work, on as many PCs as you like.

Why is the installer 51 MB?

It carries its own copy of the .NET runtime, so you don't have to install anything else first and it works the same on every PC.

Which music apps work?

Anything that shows up in the Windows media controls (the box above the volume slider when you press a volume key): Spotify, YouTube or YouTube Music in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and most modern players.

Does it work on desktop PCs and multiple monitors?

Yes on desktop PCs, though brightness control needs a laptop screen since most external monitors don't let Windows change it. With several monitors, the pill sits on your main display.

How do I close or remove it?

Right-click the pill and choose Quit Seven. To uninstall, go to Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Seven → Uninstall.
